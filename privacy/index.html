Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Bucket Browser: Object Storage ("the app") is a Mac app for browsing Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2 and other S3-compatible storage. The app doesn't collect, store or share any information about you. The developer receives no data from the app.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in with the developer, and no servers of its own.

What the app stores on your Mac

  • Access keys for the storage accounts you add are stored in your Mac's Keychain. If iCloud Keychain is turned on, they sync to your other Apple devices through it, end-to-end encrypted by Apple. The developer can't see them.
  • Account settings such as account names, regions, endpoints and bucket names are stored in the app's preferences on your Mac and, with iCloud Keychain on, in iCloud Keychain.
  • Previews of files you open with Quick Look are cached on your Mac and can be cleared at any time.

Deleting an account in the app removes its keys and settings. Deleting the app removes its preferences and cache.

Network connections

The app connects only to the storage endpoints of the accounts you add, such as Amazon S3 or Cloudflare R2, to list, upload, download, rename and delete your files. Those requests go directly from your Mac to that provider, which handles your data under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.

Updates in the downloaded version

The copy of the app downloaded from this website (rather than the Mac App Store) checks for new versions, if you allow it, by fetching a list of releases from this website on GitHub Pages and downloading updates from GitHub. Like any web request, these reveal your IP address and the app and macOS versions to GitHub, which handles them under the GitHub Privacy Statement. No other information is sent, and the app doesn't send a system profile. The app asks before it checks automatically, and you can say no; Check for Updates in the app menu still works either way.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it doesn't link any data to your identity.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
