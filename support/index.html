Support

Need help with Bucket Browser, found a bug, or have a feature idea? Open an issue on GitHub and include your macOS version, the app version (Bucket Browser › About), and the storage provider you use. Please never include your access keys.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and choose Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2 or another S3-compatible service on the welcome screen.
  2. Enter an access key ID and secret access key. For R2 or other services, also enter the endpoint URL (for R2: https://<account-id>.r2.cloudflarestorage.com).
  3. Your buckets appear in the sidebar. Browse them like folders on your Mac.

Common questions

My buckets don't show up

The key needs permission to list buckets (s3:ListAllMyBuckets on AWS, or an R2 token with "Admin Read" or wider). Keys scoped to a single bucket can't list buckets; check that the endpoint and region are correct.

I get "access denied" when uploading or deleting

The key is probably read-only. Create a key with write access for that bucket.

Do my accounts sync to my other Macs?

Yes, when iCloud Keychain is turned on in System Settings › Apple Account › iCloud. Accounts and keys sync through it, end-to-end encrypted.

Where are my keys stored?

In your Mac's Keychain only. They're never sent anywhere except as signatures on requests to your own storage endpoints. See the privacy policy.

Keyboard shortcuts

⌘1, ⌘2, ⌘3 switch views. Space previews. ⌘I shows Get Info. ⇧⌘N makes a folder. ⇧⌘U uploads, ⌥⌘D downloads, ⌘⌫ deletes. ⌘[ and ⌘] go back and forward.

Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
