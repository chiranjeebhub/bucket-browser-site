Bucket Browser: Object Storage

A native Mac app for browsing Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2 and other S3-compatible storage the way you browse files in Finder.

Icon, list and column views, Quick Look previews, drag-and-drop uploads and downloads, Get Info, shareable presigned links, and multipart transfers. Your access keys stay in your Mac's Keychain, and the app talks only to the storage endpoints you add.
